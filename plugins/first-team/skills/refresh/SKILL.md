# Refresh First Team

## What this skill does

It turns the week into one dashboard I can read in a minute, and on the schedule I chose it brings the First Team files up to date without me touching them. I can't be everywhere at once, and priorities move: the person still working to last month's version is the last to find out. The dashboard shows how far I have come since my baseline, what the business needs right now and who is on it, what my peers' visible week went to, my team's blockers turned into asks, and a few things I can actually do.

## What it reads first

Work these out before step 1, then tell me what you found in one line so I can correct you.

1. **Where my First Team files live.** Read the Settings line in the project instructions. If there are no files yet, stop and tell me to run the files skill. If you cannot reach that place from this run, say so and stop. Read My baseline from the same place. If the place is reachable and My baseline is not there, skip steps 4 and 5, build the dashboard without sections 1 and 4 (each says "No baseline yet: run the files skill"), use the plan in my sources for section 2, and find the dashboard as in "Finding the dashboard" below; there is no Dashboard line to write yet, so tell me its link instead.
2. **My sources, and the dates they cover.** The sources linked to this project, the chat channels named in the project instructions, and my connected email, calendar and meeting notes. That is the whole list. The window is the last full week, Monday to Friday, unless I name another; the four counts use the last 30 days. Say the exact dates, and which sources you read.
3. **Whether the files are due.** Read how often the files update from the Settings line, and each file's Last updated line. If the Settings line says files update only when I ask, they are due only when I ask in this chat; a scheduled run leaves them alone and says there is no due date. Otherwise they are due when that much time has passed, or when I ask. Say whether they are due this time.

## Steps

1. **Read the week** across my sources, for each person on the file list. Do not search the web. If a search fails or returns only part of the period, say which, and treat that part as not read.

2. **If the files are due, bring each one up to date** from everything since its Last updated date, plus any source its Last updated line says could not be read, from the start of what was missed, in the same shape as before. Change only what moved, set Last updated to the last date you fully read through, naming any source that failed and the date it was last fully read, and add one line to the top of Changes: the date and what changed, in eight words or fewer, for example "18 Oct · New priority: Q4 hiring plan". Write "18 Oct · No change" only when you read the whole period and nothing moved; if part of it failed, still update what moved and log it as usual, then add a second line like "18 Oct · Slack not read", never "No change". Then keep only the newest ten lines of Changes. Where the files live decides how:
   - **Where you can edit:** edit each file in place. If an edit fails, say which and stop updating files.
   - **Google Drive dated copies** (the Settings line says so): write a new copy of each file with the date and time in its name (About NAME 2026-10-18 0930.md). Tell me how many older copies there are, so I can clear them out.

   If the files are not due, leave them alone and say when they will be.

3. **Find what the business needs right now:** the company's priorities this quarter: the priorities under Now in My baseline, which I keep, or if there are none there, the plan in my sources. If the plan in my sources has changed since, say so and suggest I update Now. If you have neither, section 2 says so and asks me for them, and you finish the rest.

4. **Count where I am now,** using the four counts exactly as My baseline says they are made, over the last 30 days. Compare with the baseline only where the same sources were read in full both times. Where a source was added, lost, or only partly read since, show both numbers, say which source, and mark them "not directly comparable". A source you could not reach is unknown, never zero.

5. **Check my team's blockers.** For each blocker under Now in My baseline: is it still showing up in my sources, no longer showing up, or not clear? Also say how many of the blockers from the baseline day are still showing up. Never change the Baseline part of the file.

6. **Build My First Team this week** as one page I can open, in the tool's own page builder: an artifact in Claude, a site in ChatGPT. It shows my numbers and notes on my peers, so it must stay private to me: never publish it or share its link. If you cannot keep it private, show it here in the chat instead. Update (or, where there is none, make) the dashboard found as in "Finding the dashboard" below, writing its link on the Dashboard line (for Drive dated copies, in a new dated copy of My baseline). The Dashboard line is the only thing in My baseline you ever change. In this order:
   1. **Where you started, and where you are now.** One tile per count: a ring showing the share, the number large (for example "2 of 6"), the baseline beside it once there is a change, and a status chip with an icon and a word. Promises is one ring split three ways: kept, past due, no date. Then two more tiles, as they were on the baseline day and never asked again: my memory check, and my prep minutes, actual against needed.
   2. **What the business needs right now.** A grid: each company priority from step 3 down the side, each peer and me across the top, a dot in that person's colour where they are working on it. Mark the row where the business needs me and nobody has it covered.
   3. **You can't be everywhere.** Three charts at most, each readable in a few seconds, with its numbers beside the marks and on hover. All three show only what I could see in shared meetings and channels:
      - **What the team's week went to:** one bar per area (for example planning, hiring, a launch, customers), split by person in their colour, in shared meeting hours, from meetings with a scheduled length. Never call this their time or a timesheet.
      - **Who you spend time with:** my meeting hours with each person over the last four weeks, one bar each in their colour, with our shared threads written beside it. Under it, Monday to Friday this week, the number of First Team meetings each day.
      - **What keeps coming up:** the topics that came up most across the whole First Team this week, one bar each, with how many meetings and threads mentioned it.

      Draw each measure only when its own evidence supports it: hours need meetings with a scheduled length, thread counts need threads, and topics need at least five meetings and threads. Where a measure has too little, one line on what you could see, for example "A quiet week: two threads and no meetings with your First Team."
   4. **Your team's blockers, as asks.** Each blocker from step 5 with a chip: still open, or no longer showing up. Under each one still open or not clear, one line per ask, edged in the colour of the peer who can help: "Ask Maya: where did the venue lead land?" What it costs the business goes on hover.
   5. **What to do this week, four at most.** One line each, starting with a verb, marked with the colours of the people it involves, for example "Put the Friday sync back on the calendar". The evidence goes on hover.

   **How it looks.** The page is for reading in a minute:
   - Every section title says the takeaway as a sentence, for example "Strong on the business. Behind on replies and prep." or "Two weeks without a First Team meeting."
   - About 150 words on the page, not counting what shows on hover and the folded section. No paragraphs. At most one short line under any number.
   - Evidence, dates and caveats show on hover (or a tap on a phone). Which sources were read, the dates, merged accounts and how each count is made go in one folded section at the bottom, "How this was counted".
   - Status colours, always with an icon and a word: Strong (green) at 60% or more, Watch (amber) from 40% to 59%, Behind (red) under 40%. Promises turns Watch with anything past due. Prep is actual minutes as a share of needed.
   - One colour per person, the same everywhere on the page, in this order: #2a78d6, #eb6834, #1baf7a, #eda100, #e87ba4, #008300, #4a3aa7, #e34948; me in the page's text colour. A person's name always sits beside their colour.
   - It works in light and dark mode, and on a phone.

**Finding the dashboard.** If the Dashboard line in My baseline has its link, use that. If the link is there but the dashboard cannot be opened or updated from this run, treat it as one you cannot find: never make a new one to replace a dashboard that may still exist. Otherwise look for one made in this project (in Claude, an artifact named My First Team this week from this project's chats; in ChatGPT, a site with that name from this project) whose people match my First Team, and write its link on the Dashboard line. If you looked and there is none, make one. If the files skill is running this to build the first dashboard (it has just written the person files for the first time), or I tell you I have none, make one without looking. If you could not look, or cannot tell which one is this project's: in a chat with me, ask me for the link to "the page called My First Team this week that the files skill built last time"; on a scheduled run, never make a new one, say so, and skip the dashboard this week.

7. **Tell me what changed since last week** in one line per person, whether the files were updated, and where the dashboard and the files are. On the first run there is no last week: say so and give one line per person on this week instead.

## Rules

- Read only meetings and threads where at least one person on my First Team takes part: in a channel or an email, a message they wrote or a thread they replied in (in the shared channels named in the project instructions, my own posts count too, even when nobody replied); in a meeting, they are on the invite or the notes show them speaking. Posts from bots and apps do not count. Leave out everything else in my sources: client calls, other jobs, interviews, personal meetings and classes, even when they are the most recent thing there. A peer's work outside this company (their own business, other clients, a job search) stays out too, even when it comes up in a meeting we share.
- One person can show up under more than one account or email. Merge them into one person and say which accounts you merged.
- Measure what was said and done. The four counts are about me alone. Never score anyone's tone, mood, effort or how engaged they seem.
- A quiet week is not proof of anything. Say "not found in what I could see" instead.
- Shared channels, shared documents, email threads I am on and meetings I was in only. Leave out private messages between other people, anything said in confidence, health or family details, anything my company's AI policy rules out, and passwords or logins.
- Never send, post or share the dashboard or the files. They are mine to read.
- Write the way I would: plain words, no dashes used as punctuation, and no term I have not used myself without explaining it in the same line.
